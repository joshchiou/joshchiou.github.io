  Analyze [page name] from the following perspectives. For each role, identify what's working, what's not, and specific improvements.
                                                                                                                                               
  Reader (first-time visitor, 30 seconds of attention)            
  - What do they see above the fold? Does it hook them?
  - Is the content hierarchy clear? Can they scan and get the point?
  - What questions would they have that the page doesn't answer?
  - What's boring, confusing, or too dense?

  Editor (you, maintaining this 6 months from now)
  - What's painful to update? Manual steps, fragile formatting, copy-paste?
  - What data goes stale and how would you know?
  - Are there repeated patterns that should be driven by data files?
  - Is the content portable or locked into one layout?

  UX designer (interaction, flow, responsiveness)
  - What's the interaction cost to see the content? (clicks, scrolls, swipes)
  - Does the mobile experience degrade or just shrink?                       
  - Are interactive elements discoverable and accessible?
  - Is there a clear visual hierarchy with appropriate whitespace?
                                                                  
  Storyteller (narrative arc, emotional engagement)
  - Does the page have a beginning, middle, and end?                                                                                           
  - Is there a human angle or is it just a list of facts?
  - What would make someone remember this page or share it?                                                                                    
  - Does the content show rather than tell?                       

  SEO/social (discoverability, shareability)
  - Does the meta description and title make sense in a search result?
  - Would the Open Graph preview look good if shared on Twitter/LinkedIn?
  - Are there internal links connecting this page to related content?
  - Would someone link to this page as a reference?

  Performance (load speed, asset weight)
  - Are images appropriately sized and lazy-loaded?
  - Is JS blocking the initial render or loaded efficiently?
  - Could any of this be static HTML instead of client-side rendering?

  ---
  After analysis, propose a prioritized overhaul plan. Rank changes by impact-to-effort ratio. Group into: quick wins (< 30 min), medium lifts
  (1-2 hours), and larger projects (half day+).
