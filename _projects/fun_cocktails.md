Mostly classic cocktails with occasional detours into original recipes. Current obsessions
lean towards stirred drinks: Negroni variations, spec-forward Manhattans, and anything that
involves a good amaro. I have a soft spot for funky rums and tiki cocktails.

### Favorites

<div class="recipe-grid">

<div class="recipe-card">
  <div class="recipe-card-header">
    <h4>Negroni</h4>
    <span class="recipe-method">stirred</span>
  </div>
  <ul class="recipe-ingredients">
    <li>1 oz gin</li>
    <li>1 oz Campari</li>
    <li>1 oz sweet vermouth</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="recipe-note">Strained into a coupe, orange peel expressed. Endlessly riffable — swap gin for rum (Kingston Negroni) mezcal (Oaxaca Negroni), Campari for Cynar, or vermouth for Punt e Mes.</p>
</div>

<div class="recipe-card">
  <div class="recipe-card-header">
    <h4>Manhattan</h4>
    <span class="recipe-method">stirred</span>
  </div>
  <ul class="recipe-ingredients">
    <li>2 oz rye</li>
    <li>1 oz sweet vermouth</li>
    <li>2 dashes Angostura</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="recipe-note">Strained, Luxardo cherry. Workhorse ryes (Rittenhouse, Wild Turkey 101) and fresh vermouth for an everyday sipper.</p>
</div>

<div class="recipe-card">
  <div class="recipe-card-header">
    <h4>Paper Plane</h4>
    <span class="recipe-method">shaken</span>
  </div>
  <ul class="recipe-ingredients">
    <li>0.75 oz bourbon</li>
    <li>0.75 oz Aperol</li>
    <li>0.75 oz Amaro Nonino</li>
    <li>0.75 oz lemon juice</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="recipe-note">One of the few modern classics that earns the equal-parts format. Every ingredient pulls weight.</p>
</div>

<div class="recipe-card">
  <div class="recipe-card-header">
    <h4>Jungle Bird</h4>
    <span class="recipe-method">shaken</span>
  </div>
  <ul class="recipe-ingredients">
    <li>1.5 oz dark rum</li>
    <li>0.75 oz Campari</li>
    <li>1.5 oz pineapple juice</li>
    <li>0.5 oz fresh lime juice</li>
    <li>0.5 oz simple syrup</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="recipe-note">One of my favorite tiki cocktails to make at home. I prefer using a mix of Jamaican rums like Appleton Estate and Smith & Cross. Fresh pineapple juice is key here, usually sourced from Trader Joe's.</p>
</div>

<div class="recipe-card">
  <div class="recipe-card-header">
    <h4>Saturn</h4>
    <span class="recipe-method">shaken</span>
  </div>
  <ul class="recipe-ingredients">
    <li>1.5 oz dry gin</li>
    <li>0.5 oz orgeat</li>
    <li>0.5 oz fresh lemon juice</li>
    <li>0.25 oz passionfruit syrup</li>
    <li>0.25 oz falernum</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="recipe-note">A classic tiki cocktail that feels like it shouldn't be because it lacks rum. I love this cocktail but the hardest ingredient to source is always the passionfruit syrup. One workaround is to buy frozen passionfruit juice cubes and blend with simple to make the syrup.</p>
</div>

</div>
