I am Director, Genomics at [Lilly](https://www.lilly.com), where I lead translational proteomics for obesity and cardiometabolic drug programs. I use blood protein measurements from large clinical trials to understand how drugs work. Most recently, I presented exploratory [proteomic findings from SURMOUNT-5]({{ '/projects/work_lilly_proteomics/' | relative_url }}), the head-to-head trial of tirzepatide and semaglutide, at the European Association for the Study of Diabetes (EASD) 2026 meeting. Previously, I was a Senior Principal Computational Geneticist at [Pfizer](https://www.pfizer.com), where I led cardiovascular and renal target discovery using human genetics and contributed to the [UK Biobank Pharma Proteomics Project](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/projects/large-scale-proteomic-profiling-to-facilitate-genetics-guided-drug-discovery-and-precision-medicine-the-uk-biobank-pharma-proteomics-project-ukb-ppp/) (UKB-PPP).

I completed my PhD in [Biomedical Sciences](https://biomedsci.ucsd.edu/) at [UC San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/), where I used statistical genetics and single-cell epigenomics to study how genetic risk for type 1 and type 2 diabetes acts in the pancreas.
