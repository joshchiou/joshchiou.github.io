Life in Photos
~2018
The Early Days
Los Angeles, CA

Born somewhere in Los Angeles, Claire spent her early months developing core competencies: napping at maximum efficiency, climbing things that shouldn't be climbed, and judging strangers from elevated surfaces.

February 2021
Adoption Day
Los Angeles → San Diego, CA

Adopted in Los Angeles and immediately relocated to San Diego. She handled the transition with the calm demeanor of someone who had already claimed every window in the new apartment before the boxes were unpacked.

July 2021
Cross-Country Move
San Diego, CA → Cambridge, MA

A five-hour flight across the country. Claire voiced her objections precisely once, then fell asleep and did not complain again. First encounter with East Coast humidity was less well received.

2021 – 2023
The Cambridge Years
Cambridge, MA

Settled into Cambridge life. Proximity to MIT had no effect on her publication output, but lab meetings were regularly attended from the comfort of a nearby lap. Winter coats were assessed and found disappointing.

June 2023
New Home
Watertown, MA

Upgraded from apartment to a house with a proper yard-view window. This development was greeted with immediate and sustained approval. Multiple nap locations scouted and approved on day one.
