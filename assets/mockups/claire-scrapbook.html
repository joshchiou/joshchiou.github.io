Adoption Day
📍 Los Angeles → San Diego, CA
Adopted in Los Angeles and immediately relocated to San Diego. She handled the transition with the calm demeanor of someone who had already claimed every window in the new apartment.

Cross-Country Move
📍 San Diego → Cambridge, MA
A five-hour flight across the country. Claire voiced her objections precisely once, then fell asleep and did not complain again. First encounter with East Coast humidity was less well received.

The Cambridge Years
📍 Cambridge, MA
Settled into Cambridge life with characteristic enthusiasm for window-watching. Proximity to MIT apparently had no effect on her publication output. Lab meetings were regularly attended from the comfort of a nearby lap.

New Home
📍 Watertown, MA
Upgraded from apartment to a real house with a proper yard-view window. This development was greeted with immediate and sustained approval. Multiple nap locations were scouted and approved on day one.

Senior Scientist Era

Seven years in and Claire's research program continues unabated. Current focus areas include optimized solar-patch utilization, bird behavior analysis, and the thermodynamics of lap-sitting. Her h-index remains impressive.
